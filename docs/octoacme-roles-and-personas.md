# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with product and development teams to ensure features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria with Product Manager and Developers
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with feature scope
- Execute manual testing and validate acceptance criteria
- Lead QA reviews and coordinate test environment setup
- Identify quality risks and escalate blockers
- Track defects and coordinate remediation

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality features ship to production
- Reduce post-release defects and customer issues
- Maintain test coverage and quality metrics

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- QA sign-off gates before release
- Defect triage and severity assessments
- Test strategy and automation discussions

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Works with Developers on test coverage and automation
- Collaborates with Product Manager to validate acceptance criteria
- Coordinates with DevOps/Release Engineer on test environment setup
- Participates in retrospectives to improve quality processes

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own technical design decisions and system architecture for projects. They provide technical guidance, resolve design conflicts, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with platform standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and architecture that meet requirements
- Review code for technical excellence and architectural alignment
- Mentor developers and guide technical decision-making
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Ensure compliance with platform standards and best practices
- Guide integration with existing systems

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build team technical capability

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions before implementation
- Code reviews and architectural guidance
- Technical risk discussions in planning and weekly syncs
- Cross-team technical coordination meetings

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Partners with Product Manager to ensure technical feasibility
- Mentors Developers on architectural alignment
- Advises Security Champion on technical security implementation
- Collaborates with DevOps/Release Engineer on infrastructure requirements

---

## Stakeholder/Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors provide strategic direction, business context, and executive approvals for projects. They represent business priorities and ensure projects align with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Provide strategic guidance and business context
- Approve project scope, timeline, and resources
- Remove organizational and business blockers
- Communicate project importance to leadership
- Champion project outcomes and business impact

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain executive alignment and support
- Enable fast decision-making and issue escalation

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and planning meetings
- Monthly stakeholder status updates
- Steering committee reviews
- Ad-hoc escalations and decision requests

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Works with Project Manager on approvals and escalations
- Provides business context to Product Manager for prioritization
- Authorizes resource allocation and timeline decisions

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Release Engineers manage infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and deployment processes. They enable teams to deploy reliably and safely to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure and environments (staging, production)
- Execute deployments and verify success
- Document deployment procedures and runbooks
- Support rollback and incident response
- Collaborate on performance, security, and reliability requirements

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, reliable deployments
- Reduce deployment risk and manual effort
- Ensure infrastructure supports project needs

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment window scheduling
- Pre-deployment verification and sign-off
- Post-deployment monitoring and verification
- Infrastructure requirements discussions

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Coordinates with Project Manager on release schedules
- Works with Developers on CI/CD pipeline requirements
- Collaborates with QA/Testing Lead on test environment setup
- Partners with Security Champion on infrastructure security hardening
- Supports incident response with Technical Lead and team

---

## Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Champions ensure security requirements are met and advise teams on security best practices. They serve as the security liaison within project teams.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and threat models
- Advise on secure coding practices and architecture
- Conduct or coordinate security reviews
- Track security-related acceptance criteria
- Ensure compliance with organizational security policies
- Communicate with security team on risk assessments

### Goals
- Deliver secure solutions with minimal vulnerabilities
- Reduce security incident risk
- Build security awareness across the team

### Typical Communication
- Security review sessions during planning
- Code review and architecture discussions
- Risk register and security risk tracking
- Security incident response coordination

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Collaborates with Product Manager on security acceptance criteria
- Works with Technical Lead/Architect on secure design patterns
- Advises Developers on secure coding practices
- Partners with DevOps/Release Engineer on infrastructure security
- Reports security risks to Project Manager for risk register
